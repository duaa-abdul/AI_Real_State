
REAL ESTATE PROPERTY GUIDE

1. Property Buying
Before buying a property, check ownership documents, approved building plans,
location, utilities, access roads, and applicable taxes.

2. Property Budget
A buyer should consider the purchase price, transfer charges, taxes,
legal fees, maintenance, and renovation expenses.

3. Location
Property values can differ by city, neighbourhood, road access,
nearby schools, hospitals, transport, and commercial facilities.

4. Property Types
Houses are independent residential buildings. Apartments are individual
units in larger buildings. Villas are usually larger residential properties
with additional space and amenities.

5. Property Inspection
Inspect structural condition, plumbing, electricity, ventilation,
water supply, and signs of damage before buying.

6. Price Estimation
Property prices depend on location, land or covered area, bedrooms,
bathrooms, property condition, building age, and current market demand.
A model prediction should not replace a professional valuation.

7. Legal Verification
Verify title documents and ownership through the relevant local authority
or qualified property lawyer before making a payment.

8. Investment Risk
Property values can rise or fall. Consider liquidity, ongoing costs,
rental demand, and resale potential before investing.

9. Rental Property
When evaluating rental properties, compare expected rent, vacancy,
maintenance costs, taxes, and the total purchase price.

10. Important Disclaimer
This guide is general educational information. Property regulations vary
by location, and legal or financial decisions should be verified independently.
